Deep Learning labs

XOR, optimisers, CNNs, regularisation, transfer learning and GANs

Problem

Guided course exercises.

Data

MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, tf_flowers.

Methods

  • XOR neural network from scratch (NumPy)
  • SGD vs Adam vs RMSprop on Fashion-MNIST (PyTorch)
  • CNN on MNIST with filters and feature maps
  • Normalisation, L2 and Dropout
  • VGG16 transfer learning + fine-tuning (tf_flowers)
  • GAN on MNIST

Results

  • Optimisers (Fashion-MNIST, 10 epochs): Adam and RMSprop ~88% vs 77.23% for SGD.
  • Regularisation (Fashion-MNIST, test): baseline 84.88%, normalised 87.57%, L2 + Dropout 86.32% with a smaller generalisation gap.
